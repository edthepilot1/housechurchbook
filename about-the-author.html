About the Author

Vanessa Hensel

Author of House Church: Embracing Authentic Community

VH

Vanessa Hensel is the author of House Church: Embracing Authentic Community. Her heart is for believers to experience the kind of deep, genuine fellowship modeled by Jesus and the early church — communities built on surrender to Christ, shared life, and service to one another.

Through her writing, Vanessa encourages Christians to look beyond traditional religious structures and rediscover the simplicity and intimacy of gathering in homes, where relationships run deep and the Holy Spirit is free to work. Her aim is to equip readers with both the vision and the practical steps to start and nurture house church communities of their own.

House Church: Embracing Authentic Community is available in paperback, eBook, and audiobook.
