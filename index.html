About the Book

Embracing Authentic Community

House Church: Embracing Authentic Community guides readers in establishing and nurturing house churches. It emphasizes surrendering to Jesus and creating intimate communities like the early Christian church — building deep relationships, serving one another, and fostering an environment where the Holy Spirit actively works.

Drawing on the example of Jesus and the early believers, it offers practical steps, reflective questions, and insights to help you break free from traditional structures and embrace a fulfilling, Kingdom-focused life.

Meet the Author

Vanessa Hensel

Vanessa’s heart is for believers to experience the kind of deep, genuine fellowship modeled by Jesus and the early church — communities built on surrender to Christ, shared life, and service to one another.

Through her writing, she encourages Christians to rediscover the simplicity and intimacy of gathering in homes, where relationships run deep and the Holy Spirit is free to work.
