Embracing Authentic Community

House Church: Embracing Authentic Community is a Christian book that guides readers in establishing and nurturing house churches. Authored by Vanessa Hensel, it emphasizes surrendering to Jesus and creating intimate communities like the early Christian church — building deep relationships, serving one another, and fostering an environment where the Holy Spirit actively works.

What You'll Learn

Practical steps, reflective questions, and insights to help you break free from traditional structures and embrace a fulfilling, Kingdom-focused life.
